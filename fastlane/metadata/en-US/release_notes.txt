What the app says about plant toxicity is now written into the terms.

• The terms of service state what the botanical information is worth: it is provided for information only and does not replace the advice of a vet or a doctor.
• The absence of a warning on a plant does not mean it is safe. Household safety filters exclude what is known to be harmful, not what has yet to be established. The app already flagged this, and the terms now say it too.
• The health diagnosis and the assistant rely on an artificial intelligence model: their answers may be inaccurate and are not a diagnosis.
• When you create an account, you accept the terms by ticking a box, instead of doing so by the mere act of signing up.
• Entering without an account now starts with a screen that says what the app provides and asks for your agreement.
• The terms carry a version number. Any material change will be flagged in the app before it takes effect.

The catalogue, the filters and augmented reality placement are unchanged.
