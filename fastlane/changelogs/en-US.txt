This build is version 1.0.0, the one we intend to submit to the App Store.

Nothing is new since build 37: the catalogue-grounded diagnosis, the assistant and the translations are unchanged. Only the version number moves from 0.1.12 to 1.0.0.

So a general pass is what we need, not a targeted test. If something bothers you, now is the moment to say so: once the submission is out, the smallest fix means another Apple review.

1. Create a garden end to end: questionnaire, plant picks, AR placement.
2. Run a health scan, then a conversation with the assistant.
3. Browse the catalogue and open a few plant pages.
4. Switch the app language in iOS settings and do another quick round.
